About Destiny Laboratories

Destiny Laboratories Private Limited — a pharmaceutical and nutraceutical formulations company based in Dehradun, Uttarakhand.

Company story

Destiny Laboratories Private Limited is registered at Khata No. 244, Khasra No. 91 & 92, Central Hope Town, Vikas Nagar, Dehradun, Uttarakhand – 248197.

The company develops and markets pharmaceutical and nutraceutical formulations, with a specialised portfolio spanning women's and maternal health, bone and joint care, neurology and cognition, gastroenterology, anti-infective therapy and everyday nutrition.
