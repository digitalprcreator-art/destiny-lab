## 2. Competitive strengths & weaknesses

### Alkem Laboratories
- **Strengths:** Massive domain authority; brand trust; franchise network pages; deep product catalogue; GMP/WHO-GMP certifications displayed; strong E-E-A-T.
- **Weaknesses:** Franchise content is corporate and shallow on education; category pages generic; not specialised in any single therapeutic vertical.

### Mankind Pharma
- **Strengths:** Huge keyword footprint; franchise recruitment landing pages with clear CTAs; strong brand recall; aggressive outreach.
- **Weaknesses:** Content density over depth; thin educational value; low topical depth in niche therapeutic categories.

### Aristo Pharmaceuticals
- **Strengths:** PCD-franchise-first architecture (franchise hub + state pages + products); long-tail franchise keyword coverage; dedicated franchise enquiry funnels.
- **Weaknesses:** Lower authority; dated UX on some sections; limited E-E-A-T signals (thin leadership/certification content).

### Galpha Laboratories
- **Strengths:** Pure-play PCD franchise content engine; many franchise-topic pages (profit, process, requirements); good long-tail coverage.
- **Weaknesses:** Low domain authority; heavy keyword-stuffing patterns → vulnerable to Google's useful-content/history updates; weak trust signals.

### Zydus Lifesciences
- **Strengths:** Very high authority; excellent women's-health therapeutic content; strong schema (Organization, Product, Breadcrumb, Article); robust E-E-A-T.
- **Weaknesses:** Not a franchise-recruitment play; generalist scope → less depth on individual niche formulations than a specialist could have.

### Abbott India (reference)
- **Strengths:** Brand-led gynaecology authority (Duphaston); medical-grade content; strong citations.
- **Weaknesses:** Single-brand focus; no franchise intent capture.

**Keyword gap (the core opportunity):** Competitors split into two groups — (a) franchise-content heavy but low-trust, low-authority (Aristo, Galpha), or (b) high-authority but thin on franchise/niche content (Alkem, Mankind, Zydus). **No competitor owns both.** Destiny's plan: beat group (a) on trust/compliance/depth and group (b) on franchise + niche-therapeutic coverage.

## 7. Domain authority strategy

- **Baseline (est.):** new domain ≈ DA 1–5 (or legacy-domain history if `destinylaboratories.com` is repurposed — check the archived site's backlinks).
- **12-month target (est.):** DA 25–30 via:
  - Pharma/B2B directory listings (Pharmexcil, IndiaMART, Justdial, Practo company listings, medical directories).
  - Guest/contributor content on pharma trade blogs.
  - Digital PR around certifications, facility milestones, women's-health data (once confirmable).
  - Natural links from HCP/health content citing formulations.
- **Caution:** avoid low-quality pharma directory farms (toxic for YMYL). Quality over volume.
