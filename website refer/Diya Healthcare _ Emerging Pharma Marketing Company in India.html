Who We Are – A Pharma Marketing Company in India Built on Trust

Diya Healthcare Pvt. Ltd. is a rapidly growing pharma marketing company in India, located in Chandigarh, Punjab. We excel in the marketing, distribution, and manufacturing of top-quality pharma formulations all over India’s healthcare ecosystem. As a dedicated pharma marketing company in India, we provide three core segments through our product ranges:

  • General Range – General pharmaceutical range covering everyday therapeutic needs.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What makes Diya Healthcare different from other pharma marketing companies in India?

    Diya Healthcare stands apart through our specialization in critical care and oncology segments, our strict WHO-GMP quality compliance, transparent business practices, strong promotional support, and our genuine focus on distributor growth. We’re not just a product supplier — we’re a long-term business partner.

    How can I take a PCD pharma franchise from Diya Healthcare?

    Taking a PCD pharma franchise from Diya Healthcare is simple. Contact us via phone, WhatsApp, or the enquiry form on our website. Our team will guide you through territory selection, product selection, pricing, agreement signing, and dispatch of marketing materials. The entire onboarding process is transparent and professionally managed.

    Does Diya Healthcare offer monopoly rights to franchise partners?

    Yes. Diya Healthcare offers exclusive monopoly rights in your selected territory as part of our PCD pharma franchise program. This means you are the only authorized distributor of Diya Healthcare products in your area, giving you a competitive advantage.

    What therapeutic segments does Diya Healthcare cover?

    We cover 20+ therapeutic categories including antibiotics, cardiovascular, gastroenterology, orthopedics, neurology, dermatology, gynecology, pediatrics, diabetology, oncology, critical care, and nutraceuticals — making us a comprehensive pharma marketing company in India for diverse market needs.

    Does Diya Healthcare provide marketing and promotional support?

    Yes. We provide complete promotional support to all our franchise and distribution partners including visual aids, product cards, MR bags, reminder cards, catch covers, and doctor samples. All promotional materials are provided as part of the franchise package.

    Is Diya Healthcare’s manufacturing WHO-GMP certified?

    Yes. All pharmaceutical products marketed by Diya Healthcare are manufactured in WHO-GMP certified facilities, ensuring full quality compliance, regulatory adherence, and global quality standards at every stage of production.

    What is the minimum investment required to start a pharma franchise with Diya Healthcare?

    The investment required depends on the product range and territory selected. Diya Healthcare offers flexible options suitable for first-time entrepreneurs as well as established distributors. Contact our team at +91 9855772237 for a customized franchise proposal based on your requirements.
